Synthetic division, one step at a time

Divide a polynomial by a linear divisor like x − 3 using only the coefficients: bring down, multiply, add, repeat.

Use ^ for exponents. The divisor must look like x − 3, x + 2, or 2x − 1. Fractions (1/2) and decimals work too.
